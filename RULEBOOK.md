# Trading Rulebook: ₹20,000 account (one page)
Built from the Technical Analysis Workbook (TA), The Warren Buffett Way (BW) and Stock Investing 101 (SI). [D] = adaptation to ₹20k. Research aid only: no advice.

### 1. Size every trade from the stop, never from conviction
| rule | number for ₹20k | source |
|---|---|---|
| Max loss per trade | **2% = ₹400** (hard ceiling 5% = ₹1,000) | TA 7.2.1.6 |
| Position size | **qty = ₹400 ÷ (entry − stop)**. Example: buy ₹500, stop ₹485 → 26 shares | TA 7.1 |
| Reward : risk | target **≥ 1.5×** the stop distance, or no trade | TA 7.2.1.2 |
| Option buy (NIFTY lot 65) | premium stop **≤ 6 points** (₹400), never more than **15 points** (₹1,000); wider = too big, skip or use cash/ETF | [D] |
| Cheap OTM is not a fix | low premium = low-probability bet; only ATM/ITM, only with the trend | TA 7.3 [D] |
| Sector | max one open position per sector | [D] |
| Open risk | **max 2 open trades (≤ ₹800 total risk)** | [D] |
| Leverage | no margin funding, no position bigger than the cash allows | TA 7.4.1 |

### 2. Stops and exits
- **Every order goes in with its stop** (SL-M or GTT). No stop = no trade.
- **Never widen a stop, never average down a loser.**
- Stops beyond the support/resistance zone, not on the round number.
- At **+1R** move the stop to entry.
- Book profit at the **planned target**.

### 3. Trade with the trend
- **Longs/CE only when NIFTY and the stock are above the 200-DMA**; shorts/PE only below.
- In an uptrend buy dips; in a downtrend ignore oversold.
- Trade a reversal only **after it is confirmed**.

### 4. When NOT to trade
- The setup is not a written strategy with status TEST or better.
- **Daily stop:** 2 losses or −₹800 in a day → done for the day. Never trade to get even.
- **Drawdown brakes:** at −10% (₹2,000) from peak cut risk to ₹200; at −20% (₹4,000) stop live trading and paper-trade.
- No trades on tips, TV, Telegram/WhatsApp calls or a single headline.
- Not when stressed or unable to watch the position.

### 5. Pre-trade checklist (all YES)
1. Strategy ID written? 2. With the 200-DMA trend? 3. Stop beyond the zone? 4. Qty = ₹400 ÷ stop distance? 5. Target ≥ 1.5R? 6. Under 2 open trades, no same-sector position? 7. Daily/drawdown brakes not hit? 8. Would I take it after a loss and after a win?

### 6. Psychology
- Expect to be wrong ~40% of the time; size for losing streaks.
- Loss aversion makes you hold losers and cut winners: the stop decides.
- Profits moved to investing leave the trading account.
- After a big win or loss, next trade at normal size only.
- Paper-trade first; journal every trade.
